Support & FAQ

How SmartReco decides what to show you, what it tracks, and what it will never do.

Recommendations

Why am I seeing these specific courses?

Every recommendation is built from the "Why these?" evidence panel on your My picks page — the exact list of things you actually did (searched, viewed, spent time on, saved) that the agent was allowed to use. It never recommends based on anything you didn't do.

Why did my interests seem to "reset"?

Interest scores decay with a 48-hour half-life — something you looked at yesterday still counts, but it counts less than something you looked at five minutes ago. That's deliberate: it keeps recommendations reflecting what you're into right now rather than getting stuck on a single old session.

What does the "confidence" percentage mean?

It's a blend of how many of the requested picks actually survived grounding and quality checks, and how well the retrieved courses matched your profile. A lower number usually means fewer strong matches were found, not that something is wrong — you'll still only ever see courses that genuinely fit.

Why does refreshing sometimes take a few seconds, and sometimes nothing changes?

Building a recommendation involves real retrieval and reasoning, so it isn't instant. It also won't re-run needlessly — if your interests haven't meaningfully changed since your last refresh, you'll get the same recommendation back instead of spending a call to say the same thing twice.

Trust & privacy

Can I trust the prices and claims in a recommendation?

Yes — every price, course fact, and syllabus detail shown to you comes directly from the catalogue. Recommendations are never allowed to invent a discount, a deadline, a guarantee, or an outcome ("guaranteed job," "master this overnight," and similar claims are automatically blocked before anything ships to you), regardless of what a course description might otherwise tempt a model to say.

What activity is tracked, and why?

Searches, clicks, page views, dwell time, and course saves/enrollments — the same signals shown back to you in the evidence panel. It exists solely to build your recommendations; nothing is sold or shared, and personal details you type into search (like an email address) are stripped out before they're used to generate anything.

Accounts

What's the difference between a learner and an admin account?

A learner account browses courses and gets recommendations. An admin account can additionally manage the course catalogue and see the Operations dashboard — system health, agent activity, and cost/efficiency stats. Admin access doesn't change what recommendations you personally see.
